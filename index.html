2. Consignment Terms & Conditions

1. Authorization: The Consignor grants Rat Pack Relics authorization to showcase, auction, and sell consigned items across live streaming channels (including Whatnot) and digital storefronts.

3. Listing Window & Unsold Inventory: Consigned items are placed into a 4-stream / 30-day listing window. Unsold inventory after 30 days can be returned at the Consignor's expense or relisted upon mutual agreement. At the end of the consignment window, Rat Pack Relics may offer a cash price for remaining cards at the Consignor's request if they prefer not to take back the remaining inventory. Rat Pack Relics is under no obligation to make such an offer, nor is the Consignor obligated to accept if they choose to have the remaining cards returned.

4. Payouts: Payout balances are processed within 2 business days after live stream or online order settlement.
